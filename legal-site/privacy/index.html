Open Studio Press

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 August 2026

This policy explains how Open Studio Press (“the app”) handles information when you use it on iPhone.

Information we collect

Open Studio Press does not require an account and does not send your booklets, drafts, or library to our servers. We do not operate an app backend.

Your booklet content, saved projects, and app preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not sell personal information.

Content you create

You control the text and other content you enter. Avoid including personal or confidential information unless you intend it to appear in the finished booklet.

Exporting and sharing

When you export or share a booklet, iOS and the destination you choose—such as Files, Mail, Messages, or another app—handle the resulting file. Their privacy policies apply. We do not receive a copy.

Website requests

Opening the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use loads this website from Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process limited technical request data, such as an IP address, to deliver and secure the page under its own privacy terms. Open Studio Press does not use this visit to identify you inside the app.

The support form on the home page opens your email app with the details you entered. Your email provider sends the message directly to our support address; the website does not store a copy. Your email provider’s privacy terms apply.

Data retention and deletion

Local projects remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Because the data is not sent to us, we cannot retrieve or restore it. Exported files remain wherever you chose to save or send them.

Children

Open Studio Press is a general-audience creative tool. It is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email Margleun@icloud.com.
